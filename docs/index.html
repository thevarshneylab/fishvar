fishvar — ClinVar variant → zebrafish Umax guide

Finds the orthologous position of a human variant in zebrafish, works out which single base change is needed there, and designs guides for the ABE-Umax, TCBE-Umax and zAYE editors. Missense variants get the orthologous substitution; nonsense, frameshift and start-loss variants get a premature stop ranked by whether the transcript will actually be degraded.

Runs entirely in your browser. Orthology, coordinates and sequence come from Ensembl REST; variant records from ClinVar via NCBI E-utilities. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.

Guide finding, scoring and codon consequences are computed by the Umax Guide Designer engine, loaded here unmodified — so this page and that tool rank guides identically. No genome-wide off-target search: run candidate spacers through CRISPOR or Cas-OFFinder against Danio rerio GRCz11.

Scores are a triage heuristic, not an efficiency prediction. NMD thresholds come from mammalian work and are reported as classifications, not certainties.
